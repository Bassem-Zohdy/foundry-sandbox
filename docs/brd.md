Why this product exists, who it serves, and the business goals it must reach.

## Business goals

| ID | Goal | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BG-01 | Support staff check API health themselves instead of asking an engineer | Health questions asked in the engineering chat: from about 30 a week to under 5 a week by 2027-03 |
| BG-02 | Support staff see an outage quickly | Time from an API outage to the dashboard showing it: under 1 minute, from launch |

## Stakeholders

Support team lead (sponsor), support staff (users), on-call engineers.

## Scope and out of scope

In scope: an API health endpoint and a dashboard page that shows it.

Out of scope: alerting, history and charts, status of other services, public status pages.

## Risks and assumptions

Market and domain research was skipped by the user on 2026-11-21; this is an internal sandbox tool.
